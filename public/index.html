Discover Changbai

Where the story takes root

In Northeast China, the Changbai Mountain region brings together mountain landscapes, forests and a long tradition of ginseng cultivation. Jilin’s ginseng-growing areas include Baishan, Tonghua and Yanbian.

In the Changbai Mountain region, ginseng cultivation has a history of more than 450 years, according to the Jilin provincial government.

Meet the plant behind the name

Panax ginseng is a perennial plant in the Araliaceae family. Its native range includes Northeast China, the Korean Peninsula and the Russian Far East. It is known for its branching root, green leaves and red fruit.
